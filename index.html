Conference Talk

Fraud Prevention, ML & Human-in-the-Loop Design

2026 · Nerdearla · Santiago, Chile 🇨🇱 · 25 min · Spanish

Most fraud systems are built to take the analyst out of the loop. I propose keeping them in it: the model ranks and prioritizes the review queue instead of auto-blocking, and every decision the analyst makes comes back as training signal.

Why it matters This is how I work every day, written up as a design pattern for a regional audience of practitioners. The talk covers where full automation fails in fraud prevention, what the pattern looks like as an architecture, and how to measure if keeping humans in the loop is paying off.

Data Science Stats

Iowa Liquor Sales Analysis

2024

A deep dive into 12 years of Iowa liquor sales (~28M invoices). It covers STL decomposition, isolation forest anomaly detection with PyCaret, and Bayesian estimation (BEST) to answer two questions: does inventory diversity drive sales, and did the pandemic increase alcohol consumption? Built in R with Python interop through reticulate, and published as a reproducible Quarto document.

Why it matters Two business questions answered with uncertainty attached instead of a single number, in a document you can re-run yourself.
